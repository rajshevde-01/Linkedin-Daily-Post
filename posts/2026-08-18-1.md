**⚠️ TECHNICAL RADIANCE**  
What most teams assume: “If our VPN and SIEM are solid, we’re safe.”  
What actually happens: “Mis‑configured **Zero‑Trust** policies let a compromised credential roam free, and the breach spreads before anyone notices.”  

Identity‑centric **Zero‑Trust** forces every request to prove *who* it is, *what* they can do, and *why* they need access. That mindset should drive every certification choice.

**📌 KEY ARCHITECTURE**  
- Adopt **Zero‑Trust** frameworks (NIST SP 800‑207) as a baseline.

**🛡️ OPERATIONAL CHECKLIST**  
• Enforce MFA on all privileged accounts.  
• Deploy a **Policy Decision Point (PDP)** that evaluates risk in real time.  
• Integrate **SOAR** with identity logs to auto‑remediate suspicious token usage.  
• Regularly scan for exposed secrets in CI/CD pipelines (think the Snowflake GitHub Actions injection).
