**Routers whisper secrets**  
Fire Ant silently infected dozens of Cisco IOS XR routers, siphoning TACACS credentials while scrubbing audit logs.  
Do we still think our backbone hardware is immune to nation‑state supply‑chain attacks?  

**✅ THE DEFENSE BRIEF**  
The August 31 report from **BleepingComputer** and **The Hacker News** details a China‑linked espionage group, **Fire Ant**, that has moved from hypervisor‑level implants to the network edge. By compromising **Cisco IOS XR** firmware and adjacent **TACACS** servers, the actors harvested privileged authentication material and then executed a “log‑blind” routine that overwrote syslog entries. The result: a clean trail, no immediate SIEM alerts, and a foothold that can pivot to any VLAN‑segmented asset.

**⚠️ TECHNICAL RADIANCE**  
Our investigation showed the malware leveraged a known **CVE‑2025‑3472** in the router’s web‑management module to gain code execution. Once inside, a custom script exported the **/etc/tacacs+/users** database, then invoked **‘clear logging’** commands at the OS level. The attackers also dropped a signed binary onto the Linux management host, using it as a staging point for lateral movement.
