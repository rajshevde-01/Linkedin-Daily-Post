**⚠️ HUMAN FACTOR**  
Social engineering remains the cheapest, fastest TTP.  
In the Florida DMV breach, attackers used a single stolen police credential to access the entire **DAVID** driver database.  
No zero‑day, just a poorly scoped **privilege‑escalation** scenario.  
What most teams assume – “If we lock down admin accounts, we’re safe.”  
What actually happens – “Attackers will hunt for the weakest human link, often a low‑privilege service account, and pivot.”  
The lesson for career growth: specialize in **phishing awareness**, **credential hygiene**, and **insider‑threat detection**.  
Pair those with a **Security+** or **GSEC** to prove you can translate psychology into controls.
