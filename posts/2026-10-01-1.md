**✅ THE DEFENSE BRIEF**  
Zero‑Trust isn’t a buzzword; it’s the only architecture that survives this “inside‑out” attack.  
By enforcing **identity‑centric** policies at every hop, a compromised appliance can’t act as a privileged user.  
Deploy **micro‑segmentation** with **SASE** edge enforcement, and bind every API call to a verified **IAM** token.

**📌 KEY ARCHITECTURE**

- **Just‑in‑time** privileged access (JIT‑PA) grants admin rights for a bounded window, logged to **SIEM** and fed to **SOAR** for auto‑remediation.
