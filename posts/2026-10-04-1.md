**🔍 CONTRAST FRAMEWORK**  
What most teams assume: “If we patch SharePoint after a breach, we’re safe.”  
What actually happens: Attackers use unpatched libraries *and* mis‑configured permissions to bypass patches, then move laterally with stolen service accounts.  
The gap is not a missing patch; it’s a missing **shift‑left** mindset.
