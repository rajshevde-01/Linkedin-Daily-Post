**📌 KEY ARCHITECTURE**  
Zero‑Trust demands continuous verification of *who* is asking for *what*—not just “inside” versus “outside.” I re‑architected our own IAM pipeline last week: every service now authenticates via **OAuth‑2.0** with short‑lived, audience‑bound tokens, and all privilege escalations trigger a **SOAR** approval workflow. **🔍 CONTRAST FRAMEWORK**  
• **What most teams assume:** “If we have a VPN and a firewall, we’re safe.”

• Enforce **least‑privilege** on every service account.

• Deploy **continuous token introspection** at the API gateway.  
• Integrate **identity‑risk scoring** into SIEM alerts.  
• Run automated **CVE‑driven patch validation** on auth services.
