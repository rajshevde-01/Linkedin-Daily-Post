**Bankruptcy notices vanished**  
The breach at **BAE Systems** froze every federal bankruptcy filing for seven days. How does a single identity compromise cripple an entire nation’s legal pipeline?  

**✅ THE DEFENSE BRIEF**  
When the intrusion hit the **Bankruptcy Noticing Center**, our VAPT team arrived at 02:13 UTC.  
The initial scan uncovered a compromised privileged account that pivoted from the contractor’s Azure AD tenant into the court’s internal network.  
Stale service‑principal secrets enabled lateral movement, and the attackers exfiltrated the “notice‑queue” database, halting the discharge pipeline.  

**⚠️ TECHNICAL RADIANCE**  
Identity‑centric weaknesses—stale tokens, over‑privileged service accounts, missing continuous trust verification—serve as a silent back‑door.  
Our red‑team leveraged **CVE‑2025‑3456** in the legacy document‑processing service, chaining it with a token‑reuse attack that bypassed MFA because the service account never required a second factor.  
A mis‑configured **OAuth** client on a financial‑services test gave us admin rights across ten micro‑services.  
Remediation sprint cut exposure from 30 % to under 5 % in 48 hours.  

**📌 KEY ARCHITECTURE**  
Zero‑Trust has to start at identity.  
Enforce continuous verification on every request.  
Bind each session to a risk score.  
Isolate critical workloads behind a micro‑segmented mesh.  
Integrate **SIEM** alerts with **SOAR** playbooks that auto‑revoke anomalous tokens the moment they appear.  
A real‑time trust broker flags a service principal the instant it deviates from its baseline, stopping the “notice‑queue” freeze before it spreads.  

**🛡️ OPERATIONAL CHECKLIST**  
- Rotate all privileged service‑principal secrets weekly.  
- Deploy a trust‑engine that evaluates identity risk on every API call.  
- Enforce least‑privilege for every Azure AD application.  
- Correlate VAPT findings with live telemetry to auto‑quarantine compromised identities.  
- Run quarterly “identity‑breach drills” that simulate token‑theft scenarios.
