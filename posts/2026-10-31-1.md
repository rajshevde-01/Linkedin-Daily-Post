**Wallets whisper secrets**  
Since March 2026, every Trezor Safe 5 bought from CryptoBilis carries a hidden circuit that can transmit the 12‑word seed over a mobile network.  
Are we still trusting supply‑chain checks enough?

**✅ THE DEFENSE BRIEF**  
The implant is not a firmware bug; it is a physical addition that bypasses all cryptographic checks. The board simply reads the recovery phrase from the secure element and pushes it through an LTE modem soldered onto the PCB. In a worst‑case scenario, an attacker with a single compromised unit can exfiltrate the seed in under five seconds, giving them instant control of any wallet that ever uses that seed.

**⚠️ TECHNICAL RADIANCE**  
Our forensics team dissected a seized device and found a custom‑made RF module paired with a tiny SIM slot. The module uses AT commands to open a data session, then streams the seed as plain text. No encryption, no authentication—just raw bytes over the cellular network. The fact that the hardware vendor has not yet confirmed the breach suggests a classic “trust‑but‑verify” failure at the manufacturing stage.
