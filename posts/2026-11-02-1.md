**⚠️ ZERO‑TRUST REALITY**  
What most teams assume: “If we lock the perimeter, the attacker stays out.”  
What actually happens: “Once a token slips past the perimeter, the breach spreads laterally until every identity is trusted.” Identity‑centric **Zero‑Trust** forces every request to be verified, regardless of network location. In practice that means continuous authentication, least‑privilege token scopes, and real‑time risk‑based policy enforcement.

**📌 OPERATIONAL CHECKLIST**  
• Enforce **just‑in‑time** access for all privileged APIs.

• Rotate **API secrets** on a 30‑day cadence and audit every change in **SIEM**.  
• Integrate **SOAR** playbooks that automatically quarantine accounts after a single failed MFA.  
• Conduct quarterly **red‑team** drills that simulate token theft against your wallet services.
