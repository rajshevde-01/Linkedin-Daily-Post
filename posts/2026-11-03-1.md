**TV boxes became botnet launchpads**  
Google’s takedown knocked offline more than **1.3 million** compromised Android devices in under 48 hours.  

**✅ THE INCIDENT QUICK TAKE**  
- **Google** identified a command‑and‑control (C2) infrastructure controlling Android set‑top boxes manufactured by **Videostrong**.  
- The malicious firmware gave attackers persistent RCE, letting them enlist the devices in a massive DDoS‑as‑a‑service platform.  
- By hijacking the OTA update channel, the actors could push new payloads without user interaction, inflating the botnet to **1.3 million** nodes before the disruption.
