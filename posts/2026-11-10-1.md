**Cold chain sabotage**  
Iranian IRGC disrupted refrigeration controllers at 700 French retail locations, forcing emergency restocks and risking food spoilage.  
How many of your OT assets are truly air‑gapped?  

**✅ VAPT TAKEAWAY**  
The attack leveraged a classic PLC firmware tampering vector: attackers gained a remote shell on the controller.  
They altered the defrost cycle and left a backdoor for later RCE.  
The breach was not a phishing story; it was a supply‑chain intrusion.  
It bypassed perimeter firewalls because the devices sit on the same LAN as POS terminals.  
In our pen‑tests, we see the same pattern: “air‑gap” is a myth when SCADA networks share VLANs with business IT.  

**⚠️ TECHNICAL RADIANCE**  
What actually happens: “If any workstation on the same VLAN is compromised, the attacker can pivot to PLCs using default credentials.”  
They can also exploit known CVE‑2025‑4620 for industrial Ethernet stacks.  
In the French case, the adversary entered via a compromised vendor VPN account, then used the Modbus default admin password to inject malicious code.  
The result? A silent RCE that only manifested when the defrost schedule hit, giving the attackers a 48‑hour window to exfiltrate logs before anyone noticed.  

**📌 KEY ARCHITECTURE**  
- Enforce Zero‑Trust segmentation between IT and OT, not just VLAN separation.  
- Deploy SIEM correlating temperature sensor anomalies with login events.  
- Integrate SOAR playbooks that auto‑isolate a controller when its firmware hash deviates from baseline.  
Patch it now.  

**🗣️ PRACTITIONER MICRO‑STORY**  
Last week our team ran a red‑team exercise on a grocery chain’s cold‑storage network.  
I watched the console flash as a compromised admin account silently re‑programmed a PLC to raise the freezer temperature by 5 °C.  
Within minutes the alert queue filled with “temperature out‑of‑range” events that our SIEM had missed because the log source was mis‑tagged as “environmental”.  
We learned fast.  

**🛡️ OPERATIONAL CHECKLIST**  
• Verify every PLC firmware version against vendor hash list.  
• Rotate default credentials on all industrial devices.  

**If you still trust VLANs to protect your cold chain, expect the next breach to hit your shelves.**
