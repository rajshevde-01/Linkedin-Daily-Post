**✅ THE VAPT FINDINGS**  
During a routine **penetration test** on our new serverless stack, the team discovered an exposed **API Gateway** endpoint that bypassed **IAM** checks.  
The flaw exploited **CVE‑2023‑44487**, a privilege‑escalation vulnerability in **AWS Lambda** runtimes, allowing code execution with the **AWSLambdaBasicExecutionRole**.  
Our logs showed the attacker enumerated **15** Lambda functions, exfiltrated data.
