**✅ THE DEFENSE BRIEF**  
- **Secure Boot** must be enforced on every edge device. A disabled boot‑policy is a single point of failure.  
- Deploy **hardware root of trust (HRoT)** modules. They verify each firmware chunk against a **PKI‑signed** manifest.  
- Integrate **runtime integrity monitoring**. Use **TPM** attestation. Feed it into your **SIEM**. Flag any deviation from known good measurements.

**📌 KEY ARCHITECTURE**  
We are migrating core services to **serverless Lambdas** to cut OPEX.  
But vendor lock‑in is now a second‑order supply‑chain threat.  
If the underlying **CPU microcode** is compromised, every Lambda invocation inherits that trust deficit.  
**Zero‑Trust** must start at the silicon layer. Not just the application tier.

**🛡️ OPERATIONAL CHECKLIST**  
• Verify **code signing** on all firmware before deployment.  
• Automate **hash comparison** against vendor SBOMs in CI/CD.  
• Enable **TPM‑based measured boot** on every network appliance.  
• Conduct quarterly **hardware penetration tests** targeting supply‑chain vectors.  
• Maintain an **incident playbook** that isolates compromised devices within 15 minutes of detection.

**🔄 CONTRAST FRAMEWORK**  
Most teams assume a signed firmware image guarantees safety. Wrong.  
Sophisticated adversaries steal signing keys. They inject malicious code into the build pipeline. Signatures become meaningless.  
Relying solely on **digital signatures** without **hardware attestation** leaves a blind spot. Attackers exploit this daily.
